============================================================
 KILL CHAIN BREACH ANALYSIS
============================================================

Analyst:     Rubanpreet Singh
Date:        31 July 2026
Subject:     Customer data stolen
Source:      myfirsthack


------------------------------------------------------------
 1. BREACH SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------

a mid sized company was attacked using phishing and attacker 
established backdoor that facilitated the data theft.

------------------------------------------------------------
 2. KILL CHAIN MAPPING
------------------------------------------------------------

RECONNAISSANCE:
  attacker studied the company's website, found employees on
professional networks and researched about employees in finance
department

WEAPONIZATION:
  prepared an email with a malicious attachment disguised as invoice

DELIVERY:
   email to finance employees with malicious attachment

EXPLOITATION:
  one of the employees opened the attachment and attacker gained
initial foothold

INSTALLATION:
  attacker installed a backdoor and created a hidden scheduled task 

COMMAND AND CONTROL:
the machine connected to attacker controlled server and executed
commands remotely. gained access to credentials and found customer
database.

ACTIONS ON OBJECTIVES:
 attacker copied entire database and put for sale

------------------------------------------------------------
 3. TECHNIQUES USED (ATT&CK thinking)
------------------------------------------------------------

  Stage: Reconnaissance  ->  Technique: Phishing
        Tactic: Initial access

  Stage: Weaponisation  ->  Technique: Generate Content
		Tactic: Resource Development

  Stage: Command and Control  ->  Technique: Internal Spearphishing
		Tactic: Lateral Movement


------------------------------------------------------------
 4. WHERE THE CHAIN COULD HAVE BROKEN
------------------------------------------------------------

  Stage: Delivery  ->  Defence: scanning for suspicious files
in email
  Stage: Exploitation  ->  Defence: inspecting the file before
opening 
  Stage: Installation  ->  Defence: restricted powers for users
to create and modify scheduled tasks
  Stage: Command and Control  ->  Defence: Isolation of customer
database from other devices

  MOST EFFECTIVE DEFENCE:
  Least privilege to prevent unneeded access to resources 


------------------------------------------------------------
 5. LESSONS THAT GENERALISE
------------------------------------------------------------

   The same gaps (unpatched software, weak email
   filtering, no persistence or outbound-traffic monitoring)
   let most breaches succeed. Implementing Least privilege, 
   email filtering and regular updates would prevent this attack.

------------------------------------------------------------
 6. KEY TAKEAWAY
------------------------------------------------------------

  The attacker had to succeed at every stage; the defenders 
only needed to win once. At each stage, a strong implementation of 
security measures like filtering and least privilege help in 
prevention of majority of attacks.
